# Acta Acustica author instructions and accepted article examples

The four articles are a focused sample of original Scientific Articles accepted in 2025–2026. Acceptance dates below follow the article front pages or official publication record. The sample covers experimental acoustics, room simulation, and audio processing. It is intended to inform the structure and reporting of the manuscript.

## Requirements from the publisher

- **Article type and subject.** The Scientific Paper category covers original acoustic research, calls for relation to the state of the art, and is typically up to 12 pages. Audio Signal Processing and Transducers, Musical Acoustics, and Room Acoustics appear in the journal scope. Audio Signal Processing and Transducers is a plausible primary section for a study of signal processing and singer embeddings. [Acta Acustica Instructions for Authors](https://publications.edpsciences.org/doc_journal/instructions/aacus_instructions.pdf), §§1.1, 3.1–3.5.
- **Abstract and presentation.** The instructions specify English, a structured abstract of up to 200 words for papers, consecutively numbered pages and numbered lines on each page, and definition of mathematical symbols at first appearance. A clear initial manuscript layout is permitted. The instructions ask for numbered figures with captions grouped on a separate page and distinguishable in grayscale. They specify PNG, TIFF, or EPS for figure uploads and recommend 1200 dpi for line work. References use bracketed numbers in order of first mention and list all authors. [Instructions for Authors](https://publications.edpsciences.org/doc_journal/instructions/aacus_instructions.pdf), §§2.1–2.3, 3.5.
- **Data and code.** A Data Availability Statement and relevant data citations are mandatory. EDP encourages data and code release for standard articles. Public deposition is mandatory for audio articles and certain special issues. Distinguish externally available source corpora from derived analysis materials under the authors’ control. Make any access conditions explicit, and add a repository link once it exists. [EDP Sciences open science and journal data policies](https://www.edpsciences.org/en/open-science) and [Data Sharing and Citation Policy](https://www.edpsciences.org/en/publishing-policies-ethics#data-sharing-and-citation-policy).
- **Authors and disclosures.** The authors should confirm the author list, affiliations, corresponding author, funding, conflicts, and the auditability of the analysis before submission. The publisher asks for the AI tool, version and role to be disclosed if AI was used in writing, and requires author oversight. It restricts AI-generated or AI-modified manuscript artwork. [Instructions for Authors](https://publications.edpsciences.org/doc_journal/instructions/aacus_instructions.pdf), §§1.3–1.5, 3.3, and [EDP Sciences AI and ethics policy](https://www.edpsciences.org/en/publishing-policies-ethics#ai-and-ethics).

## Editorial choices for this manuscript

1. Lead with the acoustics question about how signal processing changes relative scores, and cite work on singer representations and recording confounds for its scientific context. The selected Acta Acustica articles support methodological context where relevant. Avoid citing examples solely because they appeared in the venue.
2. State the singer cohorts and condition assignment before presenting performance. Report both mismatch directions and describe explicitly which of the query, genuine candidate and impostor receives each transform.
3. Use uncertainty and named outcomes at the correct analysis level. Distinguish singer-level ranking errors, recording-level auxiliary diagnostic results, and descriptive mixed-gallery top-rank shifts. Pairwise ranking is threshold-free.
4. Place the fixed-design held-out response comparison and its smaller primary-setting results before the larger high-wet stress result. Mark the latter as an exploratory comparison with its exact gallery composition, and state the reference condition.
5. Preserve an inspectable link from the reported numbers to the original result files, scored manifests, and checkpoints. The illustrative accepted papers do not create a requirement to share source audio, prove perceptual equivalence, or reach any particular numerical threshold.
