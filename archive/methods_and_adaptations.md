## Interpretation

No mitigation is currently demonstrated as a robust, externally validated solution. M2 and paired adaptation merit a preregistered untouched-singer follow-up because they show substantial pilot gains. The paper should report clean error alongside shifted error, use singer-level uncertainty, and compare the raw encoder, C1, M2, and the strongest simple correction on identical frozen splits. Exact values and all seeds are in the linked raw archives.
