# Decision protocol — mixed acoustic galleries

Written before rendering and scoring the evaluation. This is a project decision gate, not a public preregistration. Earlier exploratory JVS, VocalSet and GTSinger results motivated the question and choice of model.

## Evaluation inputs

- CoVox: both pop recordings with lyrics (CH and AL) for each of the 22 documented female singers. Balance both song directions. Short recordings are centered and zero-padded to four seconds after rendering.
- NUS-48E: the 48 recordings and four-second crop starts from the existing paired experiment manifest. Sort each singer’s songs by source filename and balance all cross-recording orders. For each genuine recording, each other singer contributes the recording at the same within-singer slot. This gives one recording per impostor identity and keeps gallery size fixed. NUS title labels are sparse across singers, so this matches recording slots rather than song titles. This clarification was made after input inventory and before inference or scoring. Candidate pools are matched by broad voice group when group assignment is available from source documentation. Otherwise include all other singers and disclose this. NUS result interpretation is limited by 12 singers. NUS and CoVox have prior codec and style results respectively, but were outside the shared-reverb discovery analyses here.
- Room responses: choose four pairs from the existing MIT impulse response survey archive, excluding the eight earlier discovery files. Include common indoor room classes only, with distinct class labels within each pair. Match at fixed mean T30 targets of 0.30, 0.45, 0.60 and 0.80 seconds, with a maximum absolute within-pair gap of 0.08 seconds. Choose the eligible previously unused pair by smallest sum of absolute distances from the target, breaking ties by gap then filenames. Keep chosen rooms fixed for both datasets. T30 is estimated from a broadband decay fit after the maximum-amplitude sample. Compare exact response matching against different responses at equal wet fractions. No inference about naturally recorded rooms is planned.
- Render with the existing dry/wet recipe and power normalization. Wet fraction 0.20 is primary and 0.50 is a stress test. Use frozen Torres SSL as the primary encoder and CVSM-ART as a secondary comparison. Keep original checkpoints and audited frontend functions. Use clean cross-recording ranking and embedding checks as sanity checks.

## Endpoints and decision

1. Primary endpoint: the within-triplet rise in ranking error when the impostor response matches the query response versus the other response in the matched pair. Average with equal weights over both query response orientations, both genuine response assignments, room pairs, and query singers. The impostor identity and other audio stay fixed. This contrast standardizes over a balanced mixed gallery rather than assigning processing on the basis of identity.
2. Record the change in impostor cosine and ranking margin. Calculate paired 95% intervals resampling singer identities in query and gallery roles, conditional on the chosen set of room pairs. Report per-pair effects and leave-one-pair-out estimates as a robustness check. Check JVS and T1 archived replays through the existing scorer; the new cohorts have separate baselines.
3. Build actual balanced mixed galleries. At the singer identity level, each query's impostors are split as evenly as possible between the two room responses, with deterministic shuffled assignments and their complements. The genuine candidate takes either condition with equal weight. Report top-1 failure and the percentage-point excess of failures won by a query-matching impostor over failures won by an impostor from the other room. That secondary metric uses the same singer and room sets.
4. **Advance this as a stand-alone Q1 candidate** only if the primary endpoint for SSL at 20% wet exceeds +5 percentage points in both cohorts, has a paired lower interval bound above +2 points in both, and shows positive effects in at least three of four response pairs in both cohorts. The mixed gallery must also favor matching impostors in both. These are research prioritization thresholds fixed before scoring, not journal standards or a clinical effect. If the rule fails, close the stand-alone general mismatch direction under this design. If the upper bounds are below +5 points in both, report that the fixed-response benchmark rules out the prespecified magnitude on both observed cohorts. Large stress-only effects are a boundary of the empirical paper; they do not trigger a new method run.
