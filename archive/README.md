# Archived experiment records

This area holds the relevant historical source code, saved outputs and protocols from the two working trees. The portable commands in ../workflow are the default for reruns. Historical scripts and reports may refer to original directory layouts or mention unpublished exploratory branches. Source-result fingerprints and the corrected numerical record are in ../evidence.

The measured-response and real-mixture scoring modules accept paths to the bundled core verdicts and the isolated run folder. The shared CVSM loader also accepts the exact checkpoint prefix. Their trial construction and numerical scoring logic are preserved. Other historical scripts are kept to document the original processing and training. Exact reproduction of the auxiliary methods may require author-local training assets. The article's new methods, limitations, and current interpretation are indexed in ../docs/CLAIMS.md. Human-study material was outside the paper's selected evidence.
